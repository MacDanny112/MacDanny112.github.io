Hermes Agent — Personal Google Workspace Bridge

A private, single-user integration. Operated by the owner of the connected Google account.

What this application is

This is a personal automation tool that connects a locally-run AI assistant (Hermes Agent, running on the operator's own Windows PC) to that same operator's own Google account. It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it is not offered to the public.

It exists so the assistant can help its operator keep track of university coursework: reading the calendar and inbox to surface deadlines, and reading and writing documents the operator is already working on.

Who uses it

Exactly one person: the owner of the Google account it is authorised against. No third party has access, and no data is served to anyone else. The application requests no login and exposes no content publicly — it only runs on the operator's own machine.

Google user data this application requests, and why

  • Gmail (read, modify, send) — to find university and administrative email, surface anything with a deadline, and draft replies the operator reviews before sending.
  • Google Calendar — to read upcoming classes, consultations and assessment deadlines, and to create reminder events at the operator's request.
  • Google Drive, Sheets and Docs — to read and update the operator's own coursework drafts, notes and spreadsheets.
  • Contacts (read-only) — to resolve names to email addresses when drafting messages.

How your data is handled

All Google user data stays on the operator's own computer. OAuth tokens are stored in a local file on that machine. Nothing is transmitted to any third-party service, nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
